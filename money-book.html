Tower Money Book

How a tall building pays for itself, explained in plain language for architects.

A tall building is a large bet. A developer commits hundreds of millions of dollars to concrete, steel and glass years before anyone moves in, and most of a tower’s design decisions are also money decisions. This guide explains where the money comes from, where it goes, and how the calculator decides whether a scheme works. No finance background is needed: each term is explained the first time it appears.

Worked examples use round numbers in millions of Canadian dollars (C$ M) to keep the arithmetic easy. Chapters 4, 5 and 12 use the tool’s own 2026 Vancouver data.
