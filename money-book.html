A picture book for Program Value Visualizer

Tower Money Book

How a tall building pays for itself, one box at a time.

A tall building is a big bet. Someone spends hundreds of millions of dollars on concrete, steel and glass, years before anyone moves in. This book explains, in plain words, where that money comes from, where it goes, and how the calculator decides whether a tower works.

In the early chapters, coins are pretend money to keep the sums small. From chapter 5 on, numbers come from the tool’s own 2026 Vancouver data, and 1 coin = C$1 million.
